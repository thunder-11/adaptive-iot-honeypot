How scoring works

Behavioral model overview

Additive signals. Evidence adds the configured weight to a source IP's score. Scores are not globally capped at 100.

Passive decay. Scores decrease by {{ policy.DECAY_POINTS_PER_SEC }} points per second without fresh signals.

Rate windows. More than {{ policy.CONNECTION_LIMIT }} connections in {{ policy.CONNECTION_WINDOW_SEC }} seconds or {{ policy.PUBLISH_LIMIT }} publishes in {{ policy.PUBLISH_WINDOW_SEC }} seconds triggers a rate signal.

Caps. Failed-auth points are capped at {{ policy.FAILED_AUTH_CAP }}; short-session points at {{ policy.SHORT_SESSION_CAP }} per proxy lifetime.

Trusted sources. Whitelisted clients ignore ordinary behavioral scoring. Malformed packets and blacklist matches can still raise risk, capped at {{ policy.WHITELIST_MAX_ANOMALY_SCORE }}.

Fingerprint grouping. Normalized client identities and behavior help correlate sources; fingerprints do not prove identity or transfer risk.
